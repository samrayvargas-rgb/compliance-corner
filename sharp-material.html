Sharp material

Active bulletin

Wood with nails, broken pallet boards, banding, and scrap metal cut people on the way to the dumpster and again when someone reaches in later.

Gloves on. Point the sharp end away from you and away from the next person. It goes in the roll-off. It does not sit in the aisle.

What counts

  • Wood with nails or screws still in it. Broken pallet boards.
  • Banding, strapping, and cut metal edges.
  • Anything that can puncture a glove, a boot, or a tire.

How to handle it

  • Cut-resistant gloves. Closed-toe shoes. Do not grab a board by the nail side.
  • Carry it so the nails point down or away from your body and away from anyone walking with you.
  • Do not drag it across the floor. Do not toss it.
  • If it is too awkward for one person, get a second set of hands.

Into the roll-off

  • Walk it to the roll-off. Do not leave it by a dock, in a walkway, or against a wall.
  • Set it in. Do not throw it over the side where someone is standing.
  • Nails and sharp edges go down into the load, not pointing up at the next person who opens the door.
  • If the roll-off is full, or you cannot place it without climbing in, stop and tell a lead.

If you get cut

  • Stop. Wash it. Tell a lead the same shift.
  • A nail through a glove or a boot is a report, not a tough-it-out.
