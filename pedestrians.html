Pedestrians and forklifts

Active bulletin

People on foot struck by a truck are about 1 in 5 forklift deaths. The truck does not stop as fast as you think, and the rear end swings wide.

Stay in the marked walkway. Make eye contact. Do not assume the driver saw you.

On foot

  • Yield to the forklift unless the operator has clearly yielded to you.
  • Stop at doorways and corners. Listen for the horn. Look for lights.
  • Never walk under raised forks. Stay out of the fall zone.
  • Do not walk up to talk until the forks are down and the truck is off.
  • On a scooter: horn, and slow down at blind corners and intersections.

Operators

  • Horn at intersections and anywhere people are on foot.
  • If the load blocks your view, travel in reverse.
  • Slow in high traffic. Report a near miss to a lead.
