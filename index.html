SDS

Safety Data Sheets

Type a few letters. 57 sheets on file.

Open

Use these first

  • Section 2 — Hazard(s). Pictograms, signal word (Danger / Warning), what it can do to you.
  • Section 4 — First aid. Eyes, skin, inhale, swallow. Take this page to medical if someone is hurt.
  • Section 6 — Spill. What to do, what not to mix it with, who to call.
  • Section 8 — PPE. Gloves, glasses, respirator — what the sheet requires.

Then these on the job

  • 1 Identification — product name; must match the label on the container.
  • 3 Composition — what is in it.
  • 5 Fire-fighting — extinguisher type, what not to use.
  • 7 Handling & storage — where it belongs, incompatibles (AZ-204 / AZ-190).
  • 9–11 — physical properties, reactivity, health effects.
  • 13 Disposal — not the trash or drain unless the sheet says so.
  • 14 Transport — UN number / shipping name if it leaves the building.
  • 12, 15, 16 — environment, regulations, other.

If the name on the jug does not match Section 1, stop and get a supervisor. No SDS on file → do not use the product.
