Floors and aisles

Active bulletin

Most slips here are not a mystery. Loose wrap, a pallet corner in the walkway, or a wet spot someone drove through.

Clear the path. Do not drive or walk through a spill. Mark it and tell a lead.

Keep the aisle open

  • No loose stretch wrap, banding, or broken pallet wood in the walkway.
  • Pallet corners stay out of the marked path.
  • Shrink wrap on the floor is a slip. Pick it up when you see it.

Wet or leaking

  • Leak from a pallet: stop, mark it, tell a lead. Do not keep working around it.
  • Wet floor from a scrubber or a roof leak: cone it. Slow the truck.
  • Food-grade product that is wet or leaking: do not put it back in storage. Separate it and call it in.
