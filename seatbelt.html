Seatbelt

Active bulletin

A tip-over is the leading forklift death. The belt is what keeps you in the seat.

If the truck has a seatbelt, wear it. Every time. Do not start until it is on.

Before you move

  • Belt on, clicked, and snug before the truck moves.
  • This covers forklifts, reach trucks, and any powered equipment that came with a belt.
  • A missing or broken belt: do not drive it. Tag it and tell a lead.

If it starts to tip

  • Stay in the seat. Do not jump.
  • Brace, lean away from the fall, and hold on.
  • Jumping is how people get crushed by the overhead guard.
