Dock and trailer

Active bulletin

A trailer that is not locked can creep. People have been hurt walking in while a truck is hooking, or standing in a trailer that starts to roll.

Do not enter a trailer until it is chocked or locked. No one in the trailer while a truck is hooking or pulling away.

Before anyone goes in

  • Dock lock engaged, or wheel chocked. If you cannot confirm it, do not enter.
  • Glad hands and landing gear set. Trailer sits level on the dock.
  • Driver out of the cab, or told to stay put. Keys are not in a truck that can pull out.
  • Dock plate or leveler seated. No gap you can step through.

While you are in it

  • No one stands between the dock and a moving trailer.
  • If the trailer shifts, get out. Do not try to hold a load.
  • Forklift in the trailer: slow, load low, watch the edge of the plate.
  • Tell a lead if the lock will not catch or a chock will not hold.

When the truck leaves

  • Clear the trailer before the driver hooks.
  • Pull the plate. Then release the lock or pull the chocks.
  • Do not release the lock while someone is still inside.
